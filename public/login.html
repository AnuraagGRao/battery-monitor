Google & Firebase Setup

To enable Google Sign-In, add either your Firebase Web App credentials or Google OAuth credentials to your .env file:

# In your .env file: FIREBASE_API_KEY=AIzaSy...
FIREBASE_AUTH_DOMAIN=your-app.firebaseapp.com
FIREBASE_PROJECT_ID=your-app
FIREBASE_APP_ID=1:12345:web:...
# Or standard Google OAuth: GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID=...apps.googleusercontent.com
